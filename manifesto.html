Manifesto

My Goals and Vision for Twic East

Twic East is not just a constituency on a map to me—it is my home, the land of my ancestors, and the place where my roots run deep. Every path, every village, and every story passed down through generations is interwoven with who I am. My identity, my values, and my personal story are forever connected to the destiny of our people.

I stand before you today guided by unwavering principles: integrity, justice, humility, and an unyielding commitment to service. I believe that true leadership is not about title or privilege; it is a sacred responsibility to serve the community that raised us and to protect the legacy of those who came before us.

Our home faces significant challenges, but it also holds immense potential. I am running for the National Legislative Assembly because I am deeply invested in our shared future. My focus is clear.

Why I Have Decided to Pursue Public Service

A Sacred Duty to Protect and Unite

Witnessing the devastating impacts of inter-communal conflicts in our region has made it clear that Twic East needs proactive, bridge-building leadership. I am entering public service to champion sustainable peacebuilding, foster local dialogue, and advocate for security strategies that keep our families safe.

Standing Resilience Against Climate Disasters

Severe flooding and mass displacement have repeatedly threatened our livelihoods, homes, and ancestral lands. I am driven to seek national representation to secure urgent infrastructure investments—such as robust dike systems, flood mitigation, and comprehensive relief networks—to safeguard our communities against recurring environmental crises.

Building Essential Foundations for Our Future

No community can thrive without accessible healthcare and quality education. The severe scarcity of functional schools and medical facilities in Twic East deprives our youth of opportunities and puts lives at risk. Public service gives me the platform to lobby aggressively for fully equipped hospitals, modern schools, and qualified personnel across every payam.

Restoring Integrity and Accountability

I firmly believe that true leadership is rooted in service, transparency, and responsibility to the people—not personal ambition. I am answering the call to public service to set a standard of open governance, where resources allocated for Twic East reach the ground and every citizen’s voice is represented at the decision-making table.

Honoring Heritage Through Action

As a son of Twic East whose identity and ancestral roots belong to this land, staying on the sidelines while our people endure hardships is not an option. My decision to run is born out of a profound personal commitment to transform our shared struggles into lasting progress and prosperity.

My Vision for the Future

Peace and Reconciliation

A future where disputes are addressed through dialogue, lawful processes, and cooperation.

Resilient Communities

Better preparation for flooding, stronger essential infrastructure, and support for affected households.

Essential Public Services

Greater attention to healthcare, education, and access to basic services.

Opportunity and Participation

More opportunities for young people and meaningful participation in decisions affecting their communities.

Delivery & Accountability
Implementation Plan
  • Engage relevant state authorities and security institutions.
  • Support inclusive peace dialogues and negotiated dispute-resolution agreements.
  • Advocate for appropriate public funding and parliamentary oversight.
Measuring Progress
  • Publish records of peace dialogues and agreed actions.
  • Report on completed conflict-prevention commitments.
  • Review verified trends in reported conflict incidents.
