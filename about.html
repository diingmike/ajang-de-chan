About Me

Meet Hon. William Ajang De Chan Awuol

A leader rooted in the soil of Twic East, guided by heritage, and dedicated to the service of our people.

A Son of Twic East

Twic East is more than a place on a map—it is the land of my ancestors, the cradle of my values, and the heart of my life’s purpose. Growing up immersed in the history, resilience, and traditions of our community, I learned early on that our strength lies in our unity, our faith, and our shared commitment to one another.

My journey has always been tied to the hopes and struggles of Twic East. From our historic payams to our vibrant youth and elders, every story shared and every challenge faced together has shaped the leader I am today.

Grounded in Values, Driven by Purpose

Throughout my life, I have been guided by core principles: integrity, accountability, empathy, and service. I believe that leadership is not a position of power, but a duty to uplift others. As a candidate for the Member of Parliament representing Twic East, my commitment is to bring principled, accessible, and energetic representation to the national level.

Building Tomorrow, Together

I am running to serve because I believe in the extraordinary potential of Twic East. Together with our community elders, youth leaders, women’s groups, and diaspora, we can turn our shared aspirations into lasting progress.

Thank you for visiting, and I invite you to join me on this journey toward a stronger, safer, and more prosperous Twic East.

My Journey

Roots & Upbringing

I was born on January 2, 1968. My father's name is Chan Awuol Chan and my mother's name is Rebecca Lueth Wel Akau. I studied until high school before joining John Garang's army when I was 19. I fought alongside my elders and peers to liberate the country and to see that South Sudanese people have rights.

Education

I studied primary school and high school in Bor town. I graduated my masters in London, England.

Professional Journey

I worked with Sudan Medical Care in Nairobi. I've worked with South Sudan National Security.

Community Involvement

I've taught many classes as a teacher in Bor.

Personal Influences

I've been inspired by great men, like my late father and the late Dr. John Garang de Mabior.

My Values and Principles

I take pride in serving those around me to make their lives easier. I take pride in helping the oppressed in the community and being their voice when they cannot speak up for themselves. I value honesty and integrity. I have always been straight with my work and in the use of the resources for their intended purpose. I value accountability. I like to see through plans and promises made.

Integrity & Accountability

Being open about commitments, explaining decisions, and making progress available for public scrutiny.

Service to the Community

Listening to residents' concerns and treating public responsibilities as a duty rather than a personal entitlement.

Unity & Peaceful Coexistence

Supporting dialogue, reconciliation, and respect across community and generational differences.

Fairness & Inclusion

Listening to different perspectives and recognizing the needs of women, young people, elders, and vulnerable households.
