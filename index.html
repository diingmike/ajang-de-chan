My Vision for Twic East

Twic East is constituency number ten in Jonglei State and Pibor Administrative Area. Its capital is Panyagor. This is my home, and that of my ancestors. I want it to reach the glory I know it deserves. But before you can provide solutions, it is important to first know what the problem is. Below are some of the issues affecting Twic East.

Issues Affecting Twic East
Inter-Communal Conflicts
Violent clashes frequently occur between Twic East communities and with their neighboring counties as well.
Severe Flooding & Displacement
Long-term, catastrophic flooding has repeatedly devastated crops, homes, and livestock, displacing thousands of residents in Twic East and forcing emergency dike construction in towns like Panyagor.
Health & Humanitarian Crises
Escalating violence has led to attacks and looting of local health facilities, severely disrupting maternity, emergency obstetric, and basic healthcare services and increasing maternal and neonatal risks.
Protection & Gender-based Concerns
Rapid needs assessments highlight rising protection issues, including conflict-related sexual violence (CSRV), family separation, and the alleged recruitment of adolescents by armed groups.
My Proposed Approach
Peacebuilding & Conflict Resolution

Sustainable peace requires dialogue, cooperation, and accountability. I propose advocating for regular peace dialogues involving community elders, youth, women, traditional leaders, and neighboring communities.

  • Support peaceful negotiations over disputes.
  • Advocate for community-based early-warning and conflict-prevention mechanisms.
  • Push for civilian protection and impartial enforcement of the law.
  • Promote reconciliation initiatives that address the causes of recurring conflicts.
Flood Resilience & Recovery

Twic East needs a coordinated, long-term response to recurring floods. I propose advocating for sustainable flood mitigation, stronger infrastructure, and effective support for displaced households.

  • Mobilize support for the assessment, construction, and maintenance of dikes and drainage systems.
  • Advocate for improved flood forecasting and early-warning systems.
  • Coordinate with humanitarian partners to support displaced families with shelter, food, and essential supplies.
  • Promote the recovery of farms, livestock, and other livelihoods affected by flooding.
Healthcare & Humanitarian Support

Access to essential healthcare must remain a priority, even during conflict and humanitarian emergencies. I propose working with relevant authorities and partners to strengthen health services across Twic East.

  • Advocate for the rehabilitation and protection of health facilities.
  • Push for reliable supplies of essential medicines and adequate healthcare personnel.
  • Prioritize maternal, neonatal, and emergency healthcare services.
  • Support better emergency referrals and access to medical care for remote communities.
Protection & Human Rights

Every person deserves safety, dignity, and protection from violence and exploitation. I propose advocating for stronger protection systems for women, children, and other vulnerable members of the community.

  • Strengthen access to confidential reporting, medical care, and psychosocial support for survivors of gender-based violence.
  • Support efforts to prevent child recruitment by armed groups.
  • Promote family tracing and reunification for separated children and households.
  • Advocate for accountability, community awareness, and accessible protection services.

Delivery steps and accountability measures are recorded in detail in my Manifesto
